# Open Co-Scientist support

Open Co-Scientist is an open-science community project at
[open-coscientist.com](https://open-coscientist.com). Support is public and
provided by the community as time allows. There is no private support service
or guaranteed response time.

## Questions and ideas

Ask about setup, use, research workflows and early ideas in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/guy915/Open-Co-Scientist/discussions).
Search existing discussions first. Include what you are trying to do, what you
tried and the relevant software versions.

## Bugs and feature proposals

Report reproducible bugs through the
[issue forms](https://github.com/guy915/Open-Co-Scientist/issues/new/choose).
Include steps to reproduce, expected and actual behavior, and whether you used
the offline backend or a model provider. A small example helps.
Use the feature form for a concrete proposal. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before starting a change.

Remove credentials, tokens, personal information and unpublished research data
from logs, examples and screenshots before posting.

## Private reports

Report vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and conduct concerns
through [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). These private reporting
channels are for security and community safety, not general support.
